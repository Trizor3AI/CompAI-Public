# Report a security or privacy problem

If you think CompAI exposed private information or read game data while its reader was turned off, [send a private report on GitHub](https://github.com/Trizor3AI/CompAI-Public/security/advisories/new). Sign in to GitHub to open the form.

Include your CompAI version (shown at the top of `/compai settings`), what you did, and what you saw. If a reader was involved, mention which one and whether it was enabled. Leave out passwords, sign-in codes, account files and private chat messages.

For installation trouble, sign-in errors or incorrect answers, see the [help guide](https://github.com/Trizor3AI/CompAI-Public/blob/main/SUPPORT.md). To see what CompAI saves or shares with your AI provider, see [You choose what it reads](https://github.com/Trizor3AI/CompAI-Public#you-choose-what-it-reads).
