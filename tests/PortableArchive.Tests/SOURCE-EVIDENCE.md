# Selected Portable Archive evidence

The supplied diagnostics identify the active `Description` agent's owned addon as `DescriptionYTC`. Its root labels are `Data` (node14, parent6) and `View recorded data.` (node32, parent31). The selected full title is node3, parent2, component path `[30,2]`. The reader copies only these headers and the selected title. It does not traverse the body or recycled list. Truncated list titles and `New` labels do not establish registration or missing entries.

Fresh read-only inspection of the installed English game sheets on 2026-10-25 established:

- `DescriptionSection` row5 subrows0/1/2 reference `DescriptionPage` rows12/22/37 respectively, labelled The Copied Factory, The Puppets' Bunker, and Konogg's Messages.
- Those page groups contain exactly11/11/6 subrows. Each has eleven `Text` references, with its title in position0. The reader verifies the complete 28-definition allowlist and requires all full titles to be unique before matching a selection. It does not read other text definitions.
- The original supplied title identifies page12 subrow0 and `DescriptionString`226. The later YoRHa Unit Report1 title identifies page22 subrow2 and `DescriptionString`336. Only display whitespace is normalized. The public `Quest` and unnamed fields are not registration flags and are never interpreted or emitted.
- The installed ClientStructs assembly supplies `AgentId.Description` (320), but no typed `AgentDescription` or `AddonDescriptionYTC` selection struct. Exact agent-owned addon resolution, exact headers, the observed selected-title path, unique full-title matching, and two stable copies are required. Generic Description windows and the separate ArchiveItem viewer are rejected.

The exported result contains two rows: the selected public title/string reference, and its page/subrow reference. No body/lore, complete owned list, missing list, unread state, or completion count is read or supplied. A selected title may identify a public entry even when its content is locked; this is never a registration or unlock assertion. Unsupported language/layout, over-limit title text, placeholders such as `???`, unknown titles, and ambiguous catalogs remain unavailable.

The 0.45.2 report said the Bunker selection stayed on an older entry and Konogg's Messages reused an earlier observation. Fresh installed-sheet inspection found all28 full titles unique (at most34 UTF8 bytes). All public bodies were below the old1024-byte cap: at most489 encoded bytes or410 normalized plain-text bytes. The precise Bunker native-copy failure was not established by that report. Konogg1–5 contain the public name expression `split(string(gstr1), ,1)`; public ExtractText removes its result whereas the displayed body includes a character name. Exact body comparison therefore rejected those personalized views. Version0.45.3 removes the unnecessary body dependency from the existing selected-title-only result. All28 definitions are identified by title in offline fixtures; new in-game selection behavior remains a separate verification.

`Capture(out bool menuObserved)` marks the menu observed immediately after exact owned-viewer verification, before catalog/language/title reads. This lets the caller discard a prior selection when the current open viewer is unreadable. Closed or unowned viewers leave the flag false. The flag does not claim a selected title was successfully identified.

The 0.45.1 human diagnostic isolated a rejection of the visible `Data` heading at `text_length_metadata`. Inspection of the installed Utf8String getter IL confirms `Length = Max(0, BufUsed - 1)` and `AsSpan` uses that length, without reading `StringLength`. The 0.45.2 correction follows the actual bounded bytes, retaining capacity, terminator and payload validation, detached copying, and repeated pointer/size/byte stability checks. The regression exercises valid bytes with inconsistent `StringLength` as well as malformed buffers; it does not establish the full archive's unlock state.
