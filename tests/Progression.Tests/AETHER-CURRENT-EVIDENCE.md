# Aether-current positive-count mapping

The installed API 15 `FFXIVClientStructs.xml` documents `PlayerState.IsAetherCurrentUnlocked(uint)` as checking whether a specific current has been discovered. Its argument is the full `AetherCurrent` sheet RowId, an EventId. `IsAetherCurrentZoneComplete(uint)` separately accepts the `AetherCurrentCompFlgSet` RowId. The reader passes each identifier unchanged to its documented API; it does not derive or index a current bit position.

Installed typed public definitions supply `AetherCurrentCompFlgSet.Territory` and its `AetherCurrents` collection, plus `TerritoryType.PlaceName`. The reader validates nonzero member references against the actual `AetherCurrent` sheet. Zero slots are placeholders; they do not contribute to the denominator. A zone has at most 15 member slots. Unknown or unsupported zone IDs do not call either native API and retain null counts. Public Quest references are not used to infer discoveries.

The production catalog builder was checked against the installed English public sheets during the 0.46.0 focused fixture run: 31 named zones, 303 member references, with per-zone membership counts 1, 5, 9 or 15. Empty placeholder rows were excluded, and every counted member resolved in the current AetherCurrent sheet. These are public catalog totals, not player discoveries.

The pinned installed structure has 448 current bits in 56 bytes and 31 zone bits in 4 bytes. Tests instantiate an empty managed structure to check those bounds without calling a game instance. Whole bounded byte copies serve only as equality fingerprints around the read; their bits are not interpreted. The reader checks player address/content identity before and after catalog loading and both full native-read passes. Catalog caching is bound to all four raw sheets, game-data identity and language.

`Progress` counts native positive member observations. `Required` is the validated public membership count, between 1 and 15. Zero progress means no positive observations, not zero actual discoveries. False results remain unknown. Only the independent zone flag establishes completion: an unconfirmed zone with every member positive remains unconfirmed. A completed zone with fewer positive members is inconsistent and unavailable. Repeated changed flags, unloaded state, malformed catalogs and ownership changes also remain unavailable.

Focused fixtures exercise full EventIds, sparse references, varying member totals, invalid references, unknown zones, zero/partial/all-positive observations, contradictory completion, snapshot contract rejection and capture stability. The optional `--verify-public-catalog <sqpack>` path loads installed public sheets only and checks the production catalog builder; it does not read a running game, character, account or network. The regular `--produce-fixtures <path>` output includes count-bearing rows and the unavailable/unknown boundaries for Python parity.

These checks establish typed mapping and conservative projection, not live-game negative readiness or exact undiscovered counts. Mount speed, current locations, menu numbering and travel actions are outside this change.
