# Credits

[Trizor3](https://github.com/Trizor3AI) maintains CompAI. Thanks to the people behind the tools and community resources that make it possible.

- **Running the plugin.** XIVLauncher and [Dalamud](https://github.com/goatcorp/Dalamud) bring plugins into FFXIV. [Dear ImGui](https://github.com/ocornut/imgui) supplies the controls you see in chat and settings.
- **Reading game data.** [FFXIVClientStructs](https://github.com/aers/FFXIVClientStructs), [Lumina](https://github.com/NotAdam/Lumina) and [Lumina.Excel](https://github.com/NotAdam/Lumina.Excel) help CompAI read the game information you choose to share.
- **Chat.** [OpenAI Codex](https://github.com/openai/codex) and [Grok Build](https://github.com/xai-org/grok-build) connect CompAI to your chosen AI account. [Python](https://www.python.org/) and [.NET](https://dotnet.microsoft.com/) run the companion and plugin.
- **Optional online lookups.** [XIVAPI](https://v2.xivapi.com/docs/guides/search/) provides game data, [Universalis](https://universalis.app/) contributors share market prices, and [xivgear](https://github.com/xiv-gear-planner/gear-planner) provides shared gear plans. Market prices reflect what players have reported.

Game content and trademarks belong to their respective owners, including Square Enix. CompAI is an independent project and has no endorsement from Square Enix or the projects credited here.

- **Build and provider tools.** [Dalamud.NET.Sdk](https://github.com/goatcorp/Dalamud.NET.Sdk) and [DalamudPackager](https://github.com/goatcorp/DalamudPackager) build and package CompAI. During development, [GitHub Copilot CLI](https://github.com/github/copilot-cli) was evaluated and the [Copilot SDK](https://github.com/github/copilot-sdk) served as a protocol reference. Neither ships in the player download.
- **Collections and Gold Saucer.** Card registrations, battle-companion progression, PvP series rewards and Free Company details draw on FFXIVClientStructs and Lumina. Dalamud’s UnlockState and Recollection’s [Occult Record adapter](https://github.com/zodiia/BetterAchievements/blob/c49bb2809871da410780bcdafb5325deb1e9d526/Recollection/Data/Unlockable/UnlockableOccultRecord.cs) helped check the Occult Record mapping. Collection tools and Yo-kai use game sheets and documented helpers. Square Enix’s [Triple Triad guide](https://na.finalfantasyxiv.com/lodestone/playguide/contentsguide/goldsaucer/tripletriad/) helped confirm the deck count. The [Gold Saucer reader notes](tests/Activities.Tests/SOURCE-EVIDENCE.md) cover how the deck and racing data were checked.
- **Shops.** ECommons’ [currency exchange](https://github.com/NightmareXIV/ECommons/blob/3ebb7c7911c667b89da450d527423f938f15466c/ECommons/UIHelpers/AddonMasterImplementations/ShopExchangeCurrency.cs) and [FC credit shop](https://github.com/NightmareXIV/ECommons/blob/3ebb7c7911c667b89da450d527423f938f15466c/ECommons/UIHelpers/AddonMasterImplementations/FreeCompanyCreditShop.cs) mappings and the [ADS shop runtime](https://github.com/McVaxius/ADS/blob/1d5ad22a9c09fcccd628e1044560c371df6e1f67/ADS/Services/ShopPurchaseRuntime.cs) helped identify what shops display. CompAI uses these as references for reading shops. No transaction code from these projects ships with CompAI.
- **Mogpendium.** The xivdev contributors’ [EXDSchema CSBonusSeason schema](https://github.com/xivdev/EXDSchema/blob/a1e24a0c72058353f30f23beb996792cdb926868/CSBonusSeason.yml) and Haselnussbomber and contributors’ [MogMogCheck SpecialShopService](https://github.com/Haselnussbomber/MogMogCheck/blob/2261b6b08ec7509a78e5741eb9f9123f7e9d1c4c/MogMogCheck/Services/SpecialShopService.cs) helped identify the second event currency. MogMogCheck publishes its source under AGPL-3.0. No license grant was found for the referenced EXDSchema revision. Its schema was used as a factual reference.
- **Logs and quests.** [Hunty](https://github.com/Infiziert90/Hunty) helped check Hunting Log books, [EurekaTrackerAutoPopper](https://github.com/Infiziert90/EurekaTrackerAutoPopper) and [VERMAXION](https://github.com/McVaxius/VERMAXION) helped check individual Challenge Log counters, and [Recollection](https://github.com/zodiia/BetterAchievements) and [Charon](https://github.com/ofnature/Charon) helped check Variant notebook flags. Haselnussbomber’s [HaselDebug](https://github.com/Haselnussbomber/HaselDebug/tree/91b4e56b626d0347edf22f3825b187ac07bc5996) demonstrated how quest journal entries map to game text. Dalamud’s [SamplePlugin](https://github.com/goatcorp/SamplePlugin) and documentation also served as development references.
- **Inventory and storage.** [Allagan Tools](https://github.com/Critical-Impact/InventoryTools/blob/70a9f410d9a8ab97bef6bcf795415ea2d7c75097/InventoryTools/Overlays/InventoryLargeOverlay.cs) and [CriticalCommonLib](https://github.com/Critical-Impact/CriticalCommonLib/blob/34d364ea938e585b4f7eeab5b36e4261fdd817d0/Services/Ui/AtkInventoryLarge.cs) helped check the inventory overlay layout. Critical-Impact’s [CriticalCommonLib InventoryScanner](https://github.com/Critical-Impact/CriticalCommonLib/blob/main/Services/InventoryScanner.cs) helped check container sizes and housing storage. These projects served as references while CompAI’s readers were developed.
- **Gear plans.** The xivgear project’s [API documentation](https://github.com/xiv-gear-planner/gear-planner/blob/main/API_DOC.md), [export contracts](https://github.com/xiv-gear-planner/gear-planner/blob/main/packages/xivmath/src/geartypes.ts), [stat-cap formula](https://github.com/xiv-gear-planner/gear-planner/blob/main/packages/xivmath/src/xivmath.ts) and [item-stat loading](https://github.com/xiv-gear-planner/gear-planner/blob/main/packages/core/src/datamanager_new.ts) informed how CompAI reads shared plans and interprets stats. The xivgear service supplies the plan calculations. CompAI does not bundle xivgear code.
- **Item sources.** The item guide credits Square Enix’s Eorzea Database and the contributors to the [FFXIV Console Games Wiki](https://ffxiv.consolegameswiki.com/wiki/Phantom_Dispeller_%CE%B2). The dated references are listed with each [item](bridge/item_sources.json).
